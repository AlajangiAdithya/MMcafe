## Pillar/hub pages (one-time, ship in Phase 1–2, not monthly)
- Rewrite each **hub** (`/store`, `/workshop`, `/consultancy`) as a keyword-targeted landing page with: H1 on the head term, intro answer block, "Guides" section linking spokes, FAQ block (+`FAQPage` schema), clear CTA.
- Build the **/about** page into a real E-E-A-T asset: founder + head roaster + lead trainer bios with credentials and photos (powers `author` schema across articles).

## Publishing standards (every post)
- 900–1,800 words depending on intent; answer-first; scannable (H2/H3, tables, lists).
- 1 original/real image min (alt text, WebP, dimensions set to avoid CLS).
- Internal links: ≥1 up to pillar hub, 1–2 to siblings, 1 to a money page where natural.
- `Article` + `Person` author schema; add `FAQPage` if it answers discrete questions.
- Reviewed by someone with real expertise (named) → reinforces E-E-A-T.

## Ongoing (after month 6)
- Maintain 4/mo; refresh top performers quarterly (`seo-drift` to catch regressions).
- Expand winning clusters; add comparison pages (`seo-competitor-pages`) e.g. "Mastermind vs [roaster]" only where genuinely useful.
